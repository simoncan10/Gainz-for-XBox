3. **Prompt:** "you find here two logs of what I did, based on that I need you to do
   research on Steam and find the top 50 indie games fitting to the current
   datasheet. based on this create me a new data sheet so i can search for more
   games that fit the curent top list to recommend xbox to invest in."
   Clarifications given: blend dataset-ranking with web research; CSV output;
   purpose is cross-referencing + statistical analysis for **Xbox Game Pass fit**.

   **Action:**
   - **Data bug found and fixed.** `data/raw/super raw/games.csv` embeds JSON in
     `price_overview` using *backslash*-escaped quotes (`\"`), not CSV-standard
     doubled quotes. `csv.DictReader` without `escapechar="\\"` silently
     misaligns the `name` / `type` columns on ~13% of rows. Symptom: the `type`
     column returned values like `"currency": "RUB"` and every canonical indie
     hit (Stardew Valley, Hollow Knight, Terraria, Hades, Balatro...) disappeared
     from the indie set. With the fix, `type` parses cleanly (122,191 `game` /
     17,891 `demo`) and the indie universe goes from 29,142 to 85,632 rows.
     `pipeline.py` is unaffected (it never reads `games.csv`), but any future
     script that does must pass `escapechar="\\"`.
   - Wrote `src/build_top50_datasheet.py` (stdlib only, deterministic, same
     reproducible-from-`data/raw` ethos as `pipeline.py`).
   - **Web research** on 2025-2026 Steam indie breakouts (newer than the ~Dec 2024
     raw snapshot), curated into `data/reference/web_indie_2025_2026.csv` —
     30 titles, one source URL per row so every figure is traceable. Sources:
     SteamData 2025 review, TechSpot on Steam's 2025 best-seller tiers, Kotaku,
     TheGamer (8 indies over 100k CCU in 2025), OutrunGaming's 25 best indies of
     2025, VoxBooster 2026 indie statistics, Windows Central (Meccha Chameleon),
     GamesRadar (Mina the Hollower), byKron 2026 launch figures, ScreenRant and
     GameTubing for 2026 titles.
   - **Blended top 50** = 30 web-researched 2025/2026 titles + the top 20 from the
     snapshot by a composite performance score
     (0.35·z(log owners) + 0.35·z(log reviews) + 0.30·z(review_score), pool =
     indie games with >=5,000 reviews). Five snapshot entries were manually
     excluded with documented reasons (Wallpaper Engine = utility software,
     Rocket League = Epic-owned, etc.); the exclusion list is in the script.
     Web rows only carry snapshot metrics when the matching app had >=1,000
     reviews in Dec 2024 — otherwise the row is a pre-release stub and the fields
     are left blank rather than filled with misleading numbers.
   - **Match profile**: naive-Bayes lift table comparing a 300-game "winner
     cohort" (indie, review_score>=8, >=20,000 reviews, >=1M owners) against the
     other 85,332 indie games, per tag / genre / price band. Min support 5
     winners and 50 total, Laplace smoothing 0.02 — 216 attributes survive.
   - **Candidate scoring**: full naive-Bayes log-likelihood ratio (absent
     attributes contribute too, so games aren't rewarded for merely carrying more
     Steam tags), normalised 0-100, plus a `priority_score` blending fit with
     observed performance and an `evidence_tier` flag.
   - **Validation**: AUC separating winners from the rest = **0.939 in-sample**,
     **0.950 on a held-out 30% of the winner cohort** (seed 7) — the profile
     generalises rather than memorising.

   **Headline findings (both evidence sources agree):**
   - Co-op / multiplayer is the single strongest signal. Lifts: Co-op **5.5x**,
     Online Co-Op **5.1x**, Multiplayer **4.5x**, Local Co-Op 2.6x. The 2025
     Steam charts say the same thing — R.E.P.O. (18.4M units), PEAK (15.7M),
     Schedule I (8.0M) and 2026's Meccha Chameleon (15M in under a month) are all
     co-op "friendslop".
   - Sandbox/systems depth: Moddable 4.5x, Sandbox 4.1x, Open World 3.4x, Open
     World Survival Craft 3.4x, Building 2.9x, Base Building 2.7x, Crafting 2.5x.
     VoxBooster independently reports Open World Survival Craft as the highest
     success-rate genre on Steam (20.8% of releases reach 1,000 reviews).
   - Price: **$15-29.99 dominates the catalogue winners** ($20-29.99 lift 5.8x,
     $15-19.99 3.4x) while free and sub-$5 badly *under*-perform (0.40x / 0.41x).
     The 2025-26 breakouts sit lower ($4.79-$19.99) — read as two distinct
     playbooks: durable catalogue value at $20-30 vs. viral volume at $5-10.
   - Negative signals: Casual genre 0.52x, Early Access 0.68x, Stylized 0.31x,
     Combat 0.32x, 3D 0.47x, Minimalist 0.50x.
